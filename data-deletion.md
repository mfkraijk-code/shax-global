# SHAX Data Access and Deletion Requests

Users may request access to or deletion of personal information held by SHAX.

## How to submit a request
1. Email **velionmods@gmail.com**, or open a support ticket in the official SHAX Discord: https://discord.gg/shax.
2. State that your request concerns access to or deletion of SHAX data.
3. Identify the relevant Bot feature and provide only the information reasonably needed to locate the record.
4. Never send your password, Discord account token, recovery codes, or other authentication secrets.

The operator may request reasonable verification before processing a request. Requests will be reviewed under applicable law and handled within the required timeframe.

## What may be reviewed
Depending on what SHAX actually stores, a request may concern configuration records, operational logs, ticket transcripts, moderation records, or other information linked to the requester. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

A SHAX request does not automatically delete information held by Discord, server administrators, other users, or independent providers.

## Operator handling checklist
- Verify the requester where reasonably necessary.
- Search the actual databases, configuration files, logs, ticket transcripts, exports, and relevant backups.
- Delete or anonymize eligible records.
- Record the outcome without retaining unnecessary personal information.
- Respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
