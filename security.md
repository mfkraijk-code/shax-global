# SHAX Security Contact

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in SHAX, email **velionmods@gmail.com** with a clear description and steps to reproduce the issue.

Please do not access, modify, or delete other users’ data; do not publish exploit details before the operator has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate; and do not include passwords, Discord tokens, or other secrets in your report.

Only test systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test. The operator should acknowledge reports, investigate them, and provide updates where practical.
